## Notes on major renewals

**AWS**: current contract includes a committed use discount tied to a 3.8 million dollar annual spend floor. Actual usage in FY2025 came in at 4.3 million, meaning the company is already exceeding the committed tier and paying some usage at on demand rates. Finance recommends renegotiating the floor upward at renewal to capture better unit pricing.

**Snowflake**: usage has grown 60 percent year over year, driven primarily by the warehouse scaling issues noted in the platform reliability workstream. The August renewal is a key opportunity to negotiate volume pricing, but only after the transform service rework reduces unnecessary compute.

**Meridian Health**: this is the largest non infrastructure vendor commitment. Renewal terms are typically negotiated each fall ahead of open enrollment, coordinated between Finance and People Ops.

## Contract risk flags

The PagerDuty contract auto renews unless cancelled 60 days in advance, a term Finance has flagged as unfavorable in past vendor reviews. Legal has been asked to push for a standard 30 day cancellation window at the June renewal.

No vendor currently represents a single point of failure risk requiring board disclosure under the company's vendor concentration policy, which sets that threshold at 8 percent of total operating expense for any single vendor.
