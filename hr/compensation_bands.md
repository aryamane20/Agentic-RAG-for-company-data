# Compensation Bands, Internal

Confidential. Distribution limited to HR staff and executive leadership.

## Engineering track

| Level | Title | Base salary range | Target bonus |
|---|---|---|---|
| E3 | Software Engineer II | 105,000 to 130,000 | 5 percent |
| E4 | Senior Software Engineer | 135,000 to 165,000 | 8 percent |
| E5 | Staff Engineer | 170,000 to 205,000 | 10 percent |
| E6 | Principal Engineer | 210,000 to 250,000 | 12 percent |

Equity grants for engineering roles are issued at hire and refreshed annually based on performance rating. Refresh grants range from 0.02 to 0.15 percent of fully diluted shares depending on level and rating.

## Go to market track

| Level | Title | Base salary range | Target bonus or commission |
|---|---|---|---|
| G3 | Account Executive | 80,000 to 95,000 | 80,000 to 95,000 OTE |
| G4 | Senior Account Executive | 95,000 to 115,000 | 95,000 to 115,000 OTE |
| G5 | Enterprise Account Executive | 120,000 to 140,000 | 120,000 to 140,000 OTE |

## Corporate functions track

Covers HR, Finance, Legal, and People Ops. Bands are set at 85 percent of the equivalent engineering level to reflect market data for corporate functions roles, reviewed annually against Radford survey data.

## Executive compensation

Executive base salaries and equity are set by the compensation committee and are not included in this document. See the board deck materials for that cycle, restricted to committee members.

## Merit increase guidance

Standard merit pool for the June and December cycles is 3 to 6 percent of base salary company wide, distributed based on performance rating. Employees rated does not meet are excluded from the merit pool that cycle.

## Pay equity

An annual pay equity audit is conducted by an outside firm each March. Any unexplained gaps greater than 3 percent for employees in the same level and function trigger a mandatory adjustment, funded outside the standard merit pool.
