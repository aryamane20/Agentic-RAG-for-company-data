# Annual Budget Summary, Fiscal Year 2026

Confidential. Distribution limited to executive leadership.

## Company overview

Solstice Analytics closed FY2025 with 42 million dollars in ARR, up 38 percent year over year. The FY2026 budget targets 55 million dollars in ARR, with a planned net burn of 9.2 million dollars against a current cash position of 31 million dollars, giving roughly 40 months of runway at planned burn.

## Departmental spend

| Department | FY2025 actual | FY2026 budget | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineering | 14.2 million | 17.8 million | plus 25 percent |
| Sales and marketing | 11.6 million | 15.1 million | plus 30 percent |
| Customer success | 3.4 million | 4.6 million | plus 35 percent |
| G and A | 5.1 million | 5.9 million | plus 16 percent |

## Headcount plan

Total headcount ends FY2025 at 186. The FY2026 plan adds 62 net new hires, bringing year end headcount to 248. Engineering takes the largest share of new hires at 28, followed by sales and marketing at 20.

Hiring is front loaded into the first two quarters to give new sales hires time to ramp before the back half push toward the FY2026 ARR target.

## Key assumptions

The plan assumes net revenue retention holds at 118 percent, consistent with FY2025. A drop below 110 percent would require a budget revision, most likely a slowdown in planned engineering headcount growth.

Customer acquisition cost is budgeted to hold flat at 1.4 times first year contract value. Marketing spend growth is weighted toward the enterprise segment, where CAC payback has consistently run shorter than the SMB segment.

## Board reporting cadence

Budget to actual is reviewed with the board quarterly, with a full reforecast presented at the Q2 board meeting each year based on first half actuals.
