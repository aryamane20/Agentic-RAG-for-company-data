# Solstice Analytics Employee Handbook

Last updated: January 2026

## Welcome

This handbook covers the policies that apply to everyone at Solstice Analytics. It replaces all prior versions. If something here conflicts with your offer letter, your offer letter wins for anything specific to your role.

## Paid time off

Full time employees accrue 18 days of PTO per year, credited monthly at 1.5 days. PTO carries over up to 5 unused days into the next calendar year. Anything beyond that is paid out at year end for employees at director level and above, and forfeited for everyone else.

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees get 8 sick days per year, which do not carry over. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days.

Parental leave is 14 weeks paid for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks paid for the secondary caregiver, regardless of the employee's tenure.

## Remote work

Solstice operates as remote first. Employees may work from anywhere in the country where the company is registered to pay payroll tax, currently 22 states. A list of approved states is maintained by People Ops and updated quarterly.

Teams are expected to overlap at least 4 hours with Central time for meetings. Equipment stipends of 800 dollars are issued once per year for home office setup.

## Code of conduct

Solstice has a zero tolerance policy for harassment, discrimination, and retaliation. Concerns can be raised to your manager, to People Ops directly, or through the anonymous ethics line at ethics@solsticeanalytics.com.

Conflicts of interest, including outside employment or financial interest in a competitor, must be disclosed to your manager and People Ops within 30 days of the conflict arising.

## Performance reviews

Reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Ratings run on a 4 point scale: does not meet, meets, exceeds, and significantly exceeds. Ratings tie directly to the merit increase pool for that cycle, typically 3 to 6 percent of base salary company wide.

## Termination

Solstice is an at will employer. Involuntary terminations for performance include a standard 2 week severance per year of tenure, capped at 12 weeks, plus COBRA coverage for the severance period.
