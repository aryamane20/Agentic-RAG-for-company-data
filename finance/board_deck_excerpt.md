# Board Deck Excerpt, Q1 2026

Confidential. Prepared for the board of directors. Excerpted for internal executive reference.

## Strategic priorities for the year

1. **Enterprise expansion**: move upmarket, targeting accounts above 500 employees. Enterprise currently represents 31 percent of ARR, up from 22 percent a year ago. The board has asked management to reach 40 percent by year end.
2. **Platform reliability**: reduce SEV1 and SEV2 incident count by 30 percent versus FY2025, tied to the enterprise expansion goal since larger accounts have stricter uptime expectations in their contracts.
3. **Gross margin improvement**: move from 74 percent to a target of 78 percent gross margin by optimizing warehouse compute costs, which grew faster than revenue in the second half of FY2025.

## Q1 financial highlights

ARR grew to 44.8 million dollars, ahead of the internal plan of 44.1 million. Net revenue retention came in at 121 percent, above the 118 percent budgeted assumption. Gross margin held at 75 percent, in line with plan.

Cash burn for the quarter was 2.1 million dollars against a budgeted 2.4 million, driven primarily by slower than planned hiring in customer success.

## Risks flagged to the board

Management flagged two risks this quarter. First, the competitive landscape has shifted with a well funded entrant, Datapoint, closing two competitive deals against Solstice in Q1. Second, the transform service scaling limitations described in the platform reliability workstream pose a risk to the enterprise expansion timeline if not resolved by Q3.

## Fundraising

The board discussed the possibility of a Series D raise in late FY2026 or early FY2027, contingent on hitting the 55 million dollar ARR target and closing at least 3 additional enterprise logos above 2 million dollars in annual contract value.
