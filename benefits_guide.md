# Solstice Analytics Benefits Guide

Plan year: 2026

## Health insurance

Solstice offers three medical plans through Meridian Health: a high deductible plan, a PPO base plan, and a PPO plus plan. The company covers 90 percent of the employee premium and 70 percent of dependent premiums across all three tiers.

The high deductible plan pairs with an HSA. Solstice contributes 1,200 dollars per year for individual coverage and 2,400 dollars for family coverage, deposited quarterly.

Dental and vision are bundled into a single plan through Brightline, fully employer paid for the employee, with dependent coverage available at an additional cost.

## Retirement

Solstice offers a 401k through Fidelity with a match of 4 percent on the first 5 percent of employee contributions, vesting immediately. Enrollment is automatic at a 3 percent contribution rate unless an employee opts out within the first 45 days.

## Other benefits

Employees receive 150 dollars per month toward a wellness stipend, usable for gym memberships, therapy copays, or fitness classes. This can be submitted through the Ramp expense system under the wellness category.

Commuter benefits are available pretax up to the IRS limit for employees who work from one of the company's three hub offices in Austin, Denver, or Raleigh.

Life insurance equal to 1 times annual salary is provided at no cost, with the option to purchase supplemental coverage up to 5 times salary.

## Family planning

Solstice covers fertility treatment up to a 20,000 dollar lifetime maximum through the Carrot program, available to employees regardless of tenure or plan selection.

## Questions

Benefits questions go to benefits@solsticeanalytics.com or can be raised directly with your People Ops partner during open enrollment, which runs each November.
